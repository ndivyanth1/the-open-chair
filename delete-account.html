Delete your account or data

This page explains how to delete your The Open Chair account, or delete individual saved entries, and exactly what happens to your information. The Open Chair is published by The Open Chair, Bengaluru, India.

Delete your whole account — in the app

The quickest way. Everything is removed immediately, and you don't need to contact us.

  1. Open The Open Chair app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Your data
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Confirm when asked

Your account and everything saved to it are deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Delete only some of your data — in the app

You can remove individual entries without deleting your account:

  • A daily check-in or reflection: open the check-in list and tap the bin icon beside any entry
  • Everything, while keeping your account: delete each entry individually, or email us and ask

You can also export a copy of your data first, from Profile → Your data → Export.

Prefer us to do it?

Email hello@theopenchair.in from the email address on your account, or tell us the mobile number you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We will action it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account (email, name, phone number, user ID)Deleted immediately
Daily check-ins and reflectionsDeleted immediately
Wellbeing self-check results (PHQ-9 / GAD-7)Deleted immediately
Course progressDeleted immediately
Anonymous app-usage events (e.g. "a check-in was completed") Kept, but permanently unlinked from you. These contain no health information and cannot be traced back to you. Retained up to 12 months.
Crash and error reports Contain no personal or health content. Retained up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
Brief notes from any support conversation you had with us Kept only where needed for safety or where the law requires, for up to 12 months, then deleted.

We never sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. See our privacy commitments for the full detail.
