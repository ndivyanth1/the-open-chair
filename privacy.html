Privacy

Version 1.0 · How we handle your information
The Open Chair is not an emergency service. In a crisis, call Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24×7) or your local emergency number.

We keep this simple: we collect only what we need to offer you support, we keep it secure, and we never sell it. The Open Chair is operated from Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your email address and, if you provide it, your name and mobile number. If you choose to save reflections, daily check-ins or wellbeing self-checks, we store those. We may also keep brief written notes of support conversations for continuity, safety and quality.

Where it is stored

Your account and saved entries are stored securely using Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Access is protected so that only you can read or write your own entries.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide the service — to sign you in, save your check-ins so they are there when you return, and offer continuity of support. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Analytics and crash reporting

We use privacy-first product analytics and crash reporting to understand where people get stuck and to fix errors. We deliberately do not send questionnaire answers, wellbeing scores, severity results, or anything you write. We only record that an action happened — for example, that a check-in was completed.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. Our service providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting) process data on our behalf under contract. Confidentiality is not absolute: we may disclose information where genuinely needed to protect life, or where required by law.

Your choices

You can delete any individual entry from within the app. You can also export a copy of all your data, and permanently delete your account and everything saved to it, from Profile → Your data in the app. If you would rather we did it for you, email hello@theopenchair.in.

Children

If you are under 18, please use The Open Chair with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian, in line with India’s DPDP Act, 2023.

Contact
